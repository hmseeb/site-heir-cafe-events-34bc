How much does event catering cost?

Final pricing depends on guest count, menu selections and service hours. Quotes are always free and never obligate you to book.

Are quotes free?

Quotes are always free and never obligate you to book. Free, no-pressure quotes come with same-week response, and a free consultation and written, itemized quote are part of the process.

What is included in a quote?

Your written quote itemizes the menu, staffing, serviceware, delivery, setup and cleanup so you can see exactly what you're paying for.

How long does it take to get a quote?

Tell us the date, the headcount and the vibe and we'll send a custom quote, usually within one business day. We build a custom menu and itemized quote, usually within one business day, and the typical quote turnaround is 24 Hrs.

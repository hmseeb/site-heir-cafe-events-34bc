Before you call

Quick answers

The full list lives on our FAQ section.

What should I have ready when I call?

Your date (or a range), an estimated guest count and the location. That's enough for us to check availability and give you a ballpark on the spot. Menu details can come later.

Is the quote really free?

Yes — free, itemized and with no obligation to book. We'd rather you compare honestly than feel cornered.

Can you handle a last-minute event?

Often, yes. Call rather than email for anything inside two weeks so we can check the calendar with you in real time.

Do you take texts?

We do. Text (760) 900-7350 with your date and guest count and we'll take it from there.

What about allergies and dietary restrictions?

Mention them in your first message. We prepare vegetarian, vegan, gluten-friendly and dairy-free options regularly, and we arrange separate prep for serious allergies.

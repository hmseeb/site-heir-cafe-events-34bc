Event questions

Planning details, answered

More general questions are covered on our main FAQ.

Do you offer tastings before we book?

Yes. Weddings and full-event bookings include a complimentary tasting once you've narrowed your menu. For smaller events we're glad to arrange a tasting as well — just ask when you call.

Can you work with our venue's rules and kitchen?

We work with venue coordinators regularly and are happy to review your venue's vendor requirements, load-in windows and kitchen access before your quote is finalized. If your venue has no kitchen, that's fine — most of our menus are designed to be finished on site with our own equipment.

How do you handle leftovers?

Anything safely holding at temperature is yours to keep. We'll box it up for the host or the guest of honor before we break down, and we can provide takeaway boxes so guests can carry dessert home.

What happens if our guest count changes?

Counts move — we expect it. We confirm final numbers the week of your event and adjust your invoice accordingly. Small increases can usually be absorbed even a few days out; larger jumps just need a quick phone call so we can staff and shop for it.

Do you serve outdoor and backyard events?

Frequently. We just need level ground, reachable power for the coffee bar, and shade or cover during hot weather so food and equipment stay safe. Tell us about the space when you call and we'll plan the setup with you.

How far in advance should I book an event date?

Three to six months for weddings and large events; one to two weeks is often enough for office catering, showers and smaller parties. We do take last-minute bookings when the calendar allows, so it never hurts to call and ask.

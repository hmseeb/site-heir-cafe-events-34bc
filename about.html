Our story

It started with a table, not a business plan

HEIR Cafe & Events grew out of the simplest habit there is: cooking for people and watching what happens when they sit down together. Birthdays, graduations, Sunday afternoons that ran long — the food was the excuse, the table was the point.

When friends started asking us to cater their weddings and their offices, we decided to do it properly. That meant real equipment, real baristas, food safety done by the book, and pricing you can read without a decoder. What it did not mean was losing the part that made people ask in the first place.

Today we run a cafe for the neighborhood and a catering team for the moments that matter. Same kitchen, same standards, same people — whether you're ordering a latte on the way to work or handing us a 200-guest reception.

What guides us

Four things we don't compromise on

They sound simple. Holding to them on a Saturday with three events booked is the actual work.

Genuine hospitality

Guests are people, not headcount. Our staff greet, make eye contact and remember the regulars.

Quality you can taste

Fresh ingredients, small batches and coffee dialed in daily. If it isn't good enough for our table, it doesn't leave the kitchen.

Reliability

We arrive early, we answer the phone and we do what the quote says we'll do. Event day is no time for surprises.

Community first

We're local. We cater the school fundraiser, the church potluck and the shop down the street — and we're glad to.

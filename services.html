Service 01

Craft Coffee Bar

Every drink is pulled to order by a trained barista — no pump-pot coffee, no burnt shots sitting under a heat lamp. We dial in the grinder each morning and taste before we serve, because the first cup of the day sets the tone for everything after it.

Dairy-free milks, sugar-free syrups, decaf and half-caf are always available at no extra charge, and our baristas are happy to build a drink around what you actually like.

Service 02

Cafe Kitchen & Bakery

Our kitchen runs on real ingredients and small batches. Breads and pastries come out of the oven in the morning, salads are dressed when you order them, and sandwiches are pressed to order rather than sitting in a case.

Need to feed a room instead of a table? Nearly everything on the menu can be ordered as a platter, boxed individually for grab-and-go, or scaled into a full buffet line.

  • Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-friendly options on every menu
  • Individually boxed meals for offices and meetings
  • Pastry, fruit and charcuterie platters by the tray
  • Custom cakes and dessert tables by request
Service 03

Mobile Espresso Bar Catering

We pack the whole cafe into a van and rebuild it at your venue — machines, baristas, styling and all.

We bring everything

Commercial espresso machines, grinders, milks, syrups, cups, lids, napkins and a styled bar front. You provide the space and standard power.

Real baristas

Uniformed, trained staff who greet your guests by name when they can, keep the line moving and make the bar part of the experience.

Your custom menu

Printed drink signage with your names, your event or your company logo — plus a signature drink we create with you.

Coffee bar service blocks

  • Two-hour block — ceremonies, showers and small gatherings
  • Three to four hours — receptions, parties and open houses
  • Half day — conferences, trainings and market days
  • Full day — multi-session corporate events and festivals

Setup and teardown time is ours, not yours — service hours are counted from the first drink poured.

What we need on site

  • A level space of roughly 8 feet by 8 feet
  • Access to standard household power within reach
  • About 45 minutes before guests arrive for setup
  • Shade or cover for outdoor summer events

Venue power limited? Tell us when you call — we plan around it regularly and can adjust the setup so your service still starts on time.

Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Call (760) 900-7350 — a real person answers.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and large events, three to six months is ideal because popular weekend dates fill first. Smaller parties, office catering and coffee bars can often be arranged with one to two weeks' notice, and we do our best with last-minute requests — always call and ask.

What does a quote cost, and what's included?

Quotes are always free and never obligate you to book. Your written quote itemizes the menu, staffing, serviceware, delivery, setup and cleanup so you can see exactly what you're paying for. Final pricing depends on guest count, menu selections and service hours.

How many guests can you serve?

We regularly serve groups from about 10 guests up to roughly 300. For guest counts above that, call us and we'll talk through staffing and timing before quoting — we'd rather turn down a job than under-serve your guests.

Can you accommodate allergies and dietary restrictions?

Yes. We routinely prepare vegetarian, vegan, gluten-friendly and dairy-free options, and we can label trays for your guests. Tell us about allergies during menu planning so we can arrange separate prep and avoid cross-contact.

Do you travel to our venue, office or home?

We do. Our mobile coffee bar and catering service travel throughout the local area, and we're happy to quote events further out — travel time is stated clearly on your quote, never added as a surprise. Call (760) 900-7350 with your venue address and we'll confirm coverage.

What does the mobile espresso bar need on site?

A level space of roughly 8 by 8 feet, access to standard power, and about 45 minutes to set up before service begins. We bring the machines, grinders, cups, syrups, signage and everything else. If power is limited at your venue, let us know and we'll plan around it.

How do I hold my date, and what if my plans change?

A signed agreement and a deposit reserve your date on our calendar. If your plans shift, contact us as early as you can — we will always try to move your deposit to a new available date rather than see you lose it.

How do I get started?

Call or text (760) 900-7350, email point4aj@gmail.com, or fill out the quote form. Share your date, guest count, location and the feel you're going for, and we'll take it from there.
